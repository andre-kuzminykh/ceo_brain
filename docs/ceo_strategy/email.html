1. Process Goal

Keep reply coverage, quality and speed under control across five channels (Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack). CEO Office sweeps every channel, normalises each signal, builds a Russian Telegram-summary, drafts routine replies and runs human review for important investor communications.

2. Process description
CEO Office

Step 1. Monitor channels + status sources

Sweep Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram and Slack; pull stage context from FST / NIN / FLW / LID, outreach history from Wishlists, and nearest meetings from Google Calendar.

CEO Office

Step 2. Aggregate into a single inbox stream

Consolidate every new signal into one stream to stop losing messages between channels.

CEO Office

Step 3. Per message: entity + normalisation

Determine entity (fund / contact / company / thread) and normalise the signal — source, fund, contact, role, company, AUM, date, link, event type.

CEO Office

Step 4. Source-specific context lookup

For Email / LinkedIn pull thread and templates from HubSpot. For Telegram / WhatsApp / Slack — parse manually and cross-reference against available context.

CEO Office

Step 5. Classify + suggested owner + RU summary

Classify the signal (new inbound / reply / follow-up / reminder / meeting / NDA / Data Room / intro), pick a suggested owner (CEO Office / Alina / Dima / Yohann) and write a Russian Telegram-summary with sender, role, company, AUM, gist, full translation and history.

CEO Office

Step 6. Reply drafting

For routine follow-ups / slots / reminders — build an auto-draft and send via Email / LinkedIn (manual send for Telegram / WhatsApp). For important investor replies — build a personalised draft and run mandatory human review before sending.

CEO Office

Step 7. Post-send tracker updates

Update overall status in FST (replied / reminded / waiting feedback) and the stage-specific tracker (NIN for early stage, FLW for active stage); update investor history in LID when relevant.

CEO Office

Step 8. Daily communications digest

Compile a daily digest covering who to reply, who to remind, who is waiting for feedback and which important emails still need human review.

3. Roles & responsibility
CEO Office

Single copilot loop — monitors every channel, normalises signals, drafts replies, runs human review and updates trackers.

Suggested owners

CEO Office / Alina / Dima / Yohann — chosen manually per signal.

Trackers + HubSpot

Passive stores — updated only after CEO Office completes the reply and logs status changes.

4. Systems (Tools)

  • Email / LinkedIn / WhatsApp / Telegram / Slack — five inbound channels.
  • HubSpot — thread history and templates.
  • FST / NIN / FLW / LID — fund and investor stage trackers.
  • Wishlists & outreach (WSH) — outreach history and responses.
  • Google Calendar — meetings and nearest touches.

5. Artifacts

  • Inbox stream — aggregated signals from every channel.
  • Normalised signal — source, fund, contact, role, company, AUM, date, link, event type.
  • Russian Telegram-summary — per new message, with suggested owner.
  • Reply drafts — auto for routine, personalised for investors with human review.
  • Daily communications digest — who to reply / remind / needs review.

7. Pain points
1. Five channels swept manually

Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram and Slack each require a separate manual sweep — messages slip between channels.

2. Signal normalisation by hand

Every incoming message needs source / fund / contact / role / company / AUM / date / event type tagged manually.

3. Russian summaries written one by one

Telegram-digest summaries are composed per message, limiting how many messages receive one.

4. Reply drafting is manual

Even routine follow-ups, slot proposals and reminders require writing — human reviewers become the bottleneck for everything.

5. Stalled threads and missing follow-ups surface late

Unanswered threads, overdue follow-ups and communication risk only become visible at the end-of-day digest.
