1. Process Goal

Tie goals, roadmap and performance to real pipeline movement, tasks, follow-ups and blockers. CEO Office runs a fully manual cadence — annual → quarterly → monthly → weekly → daily, plus per-meeting updates and weekly / monthly reviews — to keep goals connected to operational reality.

2. Process description
CEO Office

Step 1. Annual cycle per direction

Collect past materials, playbooks, fundraising history and external-meeting history. Shape directions (Fundraising / Partnerships / Media / CEO Office), link strategic goals to operational drivers, and set annual goals, KPIs, owners and control metrics.

CEO Office

Step 2. Quarterly cycle

Decompose annual goals into quarterly outcomes, define initiatives, owners, dependencies and key risks. Verify which pipeline metrics and processes actually drive those outcomes.

CEO Office

Step 3. Monthly cycle

Review fund stages, decline reasons, pending follow-ups and meetings held. Shape monthly focus per direction; analyse the link between fund movement, activity and goal achievement.

CEO Office

Step 4. Weekly sprint

Gather active fund statuses, intros, followers, investor touches and the week's meetings. Form the sprint backlog — tasks, owners, due dates, dependencies — for CEO Office.

CEO Office

Step 5. Daily cycle — morning / day / evening

Monitor comms (Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack), trackers (FST / NIN / FLW / LID) and calendars. Build the daily checklist, surface gaps, SLA breaches, overdue tasks, data conflicts and status desync, and emit risk signals when a goal or process is at risk.

CEO Office

Step 6. Per fundraising meeting

Fetch the Fireflies transcript, push through a GPT script for summary, follow-ups and next steps (or write the summary manually if transcript is missing), then update the fundraising calendar, fund status and add follow-up tasks to the backlog.

CEO Office

Step 7. Weekly review and retro

Cross-check meetings, summaries, fund movement and backlog. Analyse performance against goals, check risk zones (gap > 10 days, missing follow-up, no owner, stalled fund, data conflict) and compile the weekly performance review with suggested reprioritization.

CEO Office

Step 8. Monthly review

Collect period-level fund statuses, meeting history, early-stage dynamics, active-fund changes and backlog execution. Analyse the operational-to-goal link, causes of stalls / SLA breaches / missed follow-ups / data conflicts, and adjust monthly priorities, roadmap and focus areas.

3. Roles & responsibility
CEO Office

Artem, Ira, Alina and other operational roles. Run the full cadence by hand — annual → daily — and compile every review.

Goals & Roadmap / Backlog

Passive stores — updated only when someone edits them manually.

Weekly / Monthly Review

Report artifact assembled by hand at the end of each cadence tick.

4. Systems (Tools)

  • Goals & Roadmap / Sprint Backlog — planning artifacts edited by hand.
  • Daily Checklist, Weekly / Monthly Review — cadence outputs.
  • FST / NIN / FLW / LID — fund stage and investor trackers.
  • GCL / FRK — meetings and fundraising calendar.
  • Fireflies + GPT / script — transcript and manual GPT-based summary.
  • Email / LinkedIn / WhatsApp / Telegram / Slack — communication signals.
  • Confluence — historical playbooks and outcomes.

5. Artifacts

  • Annual KPI map — goals, owners, control metrics per direction.
  • Quarterly execution map — outcomes, initiatives, risks.
  • Monthly focus — priorities, deliverables, owner load.
  • Weekly sprint backlog — tasks, owners, due dates, dependencies.
  • Daily checklist — open tasks, overdue, no-owner, missing follow-up.
  • Risk signals + weekly / monthly review — compiled by hand.
6. Metrics (Observed)
Metric Observed value
Cadence Manual — every annual / quarterly / monthly / weekly / daily tick is run by CEO Office by hand.
Risk detection lag Usually one week — issues surface at the weekly retro instead of in real time.
Goal ↔ pipeline link Inferred manually — no live traceability from goal → stage → next action.
Data quality Drifts between trackers — missing fields, desync and conflicts stay silent until review.
Reprioritization Reactive — owners reassigned after the fact, rarely at the moment a risk appears.
7. Pain points
1. Fully manual cadence

CEO Office runs every annual / quarterly / monthly / weekly / daily loop by hand — planning, gathering, compiling and reviewing all happen without automation.

2. No live goal-to-pipeline link

There is no real-time traceability from strategic goals to fund stages, next actions, blockers and owner load — the link is rebuilt at each cadence tick manually.

3. Risks surface late

Stalled funds, missed follow-ups, overdue tasks and SLA breaches are typically detected at the weekly retro, not the moment they occur.

4. Silent data-quality drift

Data conflicts, missing fields and cross-tracker desync degrade pipeline quality without triggering any alert.

5. Reactive reprioritization

Owner load and priority changes happen after the fact, not at the moment a goal comes under risk — so performance loss accumulates before the team reacts.
