1. Process Goal

Produce the full reporting loop — morning / intraday / evening checklists, nothing-lost control, weekly fundraising status, executive summary, owner / process report and monthly review — by hand, pulling every signal / tracker / meeting source on each tick.

2. Process description
CEO Office

Step 1. Morning roll-up

Sweep comms channels + trackers + calendar; build morning checklist (open tasks, critical follow-ups, meetings, blockers, pending actions); write the morning executive summary.

CEO Office

Step 2. Intraday refresh

Re-check inbound and tracker changes during the day; refresh the checklist if priorities shift.

CEO Office

Step 3. Evening roll-up + nothing-lost check

End-of-day sync; build the evening checklist (overdue, no-owner, missing follow-up); run the nothing-lost check — unclosed signals, meetings without summary, stale statuses, missed follow-ups — and write the evening daily summary.

CEO Office

Step 4. Weekly fundraising status

Sync meetings, summaries, pipeline stages, intros, follow-ups, investor history, outreach and open tasks; compile the weekly fundraising status — meetings / summaries / open tasks / last touch / blockers / fund movement.

CEO Office

Step 5. Weekly executive + owner / process report

Write the weekly executive summary (overall picture / key changes / attention points) and the owner / process report — overdue follow-ups, no-owner tasks, meetings without summary, stalled funds, process quality issues.

CEO Office

Step 6. Monthly review

Collect period-level pipeline data, meetings, early-stage dynamics, active-fund changes, investor touches and execution data; analyse post-meeting dynamics and decline reasons; compile the monthly review draft.

3. Roles & responsibility
CEO Office

Single reporting loop — runs every daily / weekly / monthly roll-up and compiles reports by hand.

Daily Checklist (CHK)

Morning / evening task and follow-up store — built by hand each day.

Weekly / Monthly Report (RPT)

Compiled artifacts — populated manually per cadence tick.

4. Systems (Tools)

  • Email / LinkedIn / Telegram / Slack — daily signal sweep.
  • FST / NIN / FLW / LID — pipeline and investor source data.
  • Wishlists & outreach (WSH) — outreach context for weekly roll-up.
  • GCL / FRK — meeting calendars.
  • Daily Checklist (CHK) — morning / evening task list.
  • Weekly / Monthly Report (RPT) — report artifacts.

5. Artifacts

  • Morning checklist + exec summary — attention points for the day.
  • Evening checklist + daily summary — what is left / where is the risk.
  • Nothing-lost snapshot — unclosed signals, meetings without summary, stale statuses.
  • Weekly fundraising status — pipeline / last touch / blockers / movement.
  • Weekly exec summary + owner / process report.
  • Monthly review — funds, stages, decline reasons, post-meeting dynamics.
6. Metrics (Observed)
MetricObserved value
Morning / evening roll-upBuilt by hand — time-consuming, especially on dense pipeline days.
Nothing-lost coverageDepends on memory — meetings without summary and no-owner tasks slip.
Weekly fundraising statusCompiled by hand — Friday reporting bottleneck.
Owner / process issuesSurface only at the weekly review — late.
Monthly reviewRequires manual re-pulling of every source — slow to produce.
7. Pain points
1. Manual daily roll-ups

Morning and evening checklists and summaries are built by hand every day — overhead scales with pipeline volume.

2. Nothing-lost check by memory

Unclosed signals, meetings without summary and stale statuses are caught only if someone remembers to look.

3. Weekly fundraising status by hand

Friday reporting requires pulling meetings, summaries, stages, outreach and tasks from every source manually.

4. Owner / process issues surface late

Overdue follow-ups and no-owner tasks accumulate through the week and only appear at the weekly review.

5. Monthly review is heavy

Every monthly review reruns every source pull by hand — slow and inconsistent across months.

1. Process Goal (TO-BE)

Reports is a single agent that auto-builds the full reporting loop — morning / midday / evening control packs, weekly fundraising status + executive summary + owner / process report, monthly review and continuous nothing-lost + deviation control — and delivers alerts the moment a deviation appears.

2. Process description
Reports → COO

A. Morning control pack

Monitors comms channels + trackers + calendar in parallel, builds the morning checklist and delivers a morning control pack — checklist, key risks, top priorities, attention points.

Reports → COO

B. Midday update

Rechecks inbound and tracker changes through the day, refreshes the checklist, updates the risk state and delivers a midday update on new signals, changed priorities and emerging risks.

Reports → COO

C. Evening control pack + nothing-lost

End-of-day sync of statuses and meetings; builds evening checklist; runs nothing-lost check (unclosed signals, meetings without summary, no-owner tasks, stale statuses, missed follow-ups) and delivers an evening control pack.

COO → Reports

D. Weekly reporting pack

On weekly trigger — syncs meetings, summaries, pipeline, outreach and tasks; builds weekly fundraising status, executive summary and owner / process report; delivers the weekly reporting pack covering status, exec summary, owner-process issues and deviations.

COO → Reports

E. Monthly review pack

On monthly trigger — collects period-level data, analyses post-meeting dynamics, decline reasons and recurring process deviations, builds the monthly review and delivers a monthly review pack with pipeline dynamics, blockers, execution quality and management attention areas.

Reports

F. Continuous deviation control

Evaluates deviation rules in real time (gap > 10 days, no owner, missing follow-up, meeting without summary, stale status, blocked fund); emits alert packs (deviation / probable cause / owner / required action) or confirms clean reporting status.

Reports — Daily / Weekly / Monthly Reporting + Nothing-Lost Control

Timely deviation detection May 29, 2026 № 10

What this module does

Reports automatically produces regular management reporting on the fundraising process — daily, weekly and monthly status, checklists and owner-process reports. It collects data from meetings, tasks, touches, blockers and fund stages to surface deviations on time and keep the operational loop under control.

User stories

  1. Weekly fundraising status assembles automatically from meetings, summaries, open tasks, last touches and blockers.
  2. Get compact daily and weekly executive summary on external comms and fund stages — grasp the picture fast.
  3. See an owner / process report: overdue follow-ups, no-owner tasks, meetings without summary, stalled funds.
  4. Get a daily checklist on morning / evening / intraday updates and a "nothing lost" control.
  5. Get a monthly review on funds, stages, blockers, decline reasons and post-meeting dynamics.

Delivery requirements

  • Morning / midday / evening control packs with priorities, risks and nothing-lost audit.
  • Weekly fundraising status assembled from FRK, FST, NIN, FLW, LID, WSH and Daily Checklist.
  • Owner / process report: overdue follow-ups, no-owner tasks, meetings without summary, stalled funds, data-quality issues.
  • Monthly review: pipeline dynamics, decline reasons, blockers, execution quality, management attention areas.
  • Deviation engine: gap > 10 days, no owner, missing follow-up, meeting without summary, stale status, blocked fund.
