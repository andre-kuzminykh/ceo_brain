1. Process Goal

Turn every fundraising meeting into a structured record and track its fate — was there a follow-up, was there a reply, did the fund stage move? CEO Office writes every summary manually (or via a GPT script on the transcript), maintains meeting memory and reviews outcomes at weekly and monthly checkpoints.

2. Process description
CEO Office

Step 1. Check transcript and build RU summary

Pull Fireflies transcript. If it is available and sufficient — run through GPT script for a Russian-language summary with theses, risks, next step, follow-up and open questions. Otherwise — collect manual notes and generate the summary from them.

CEO Office

Step 2. Normalise and short-notes

Bring the summary to a single format and write short notes — what was discussed, what was agreed, what to do next.

CEO Office

Step 3. Suggested follow-up + owner

Decide the suggested follow-up, next task and owner (CEO Office / Alina / Dima / Yohann) and cross-check HubSpot for prior comms and templates.

CEO Office

Step 4. Save meeting memory + update statuses

Save meeting memory to the fundraising calendar (summary, short notes, owner, follow-up, next step), update the fund status (post-meeting, waiting feedback, last touch, next step) and add related tasks to the checklist.

CEO Office

Step 5. Within-a-week post-meeting control

Re-check meeting memory, fund status and whether the follow-up was sent. Create alarms for missed actions, switch to waiting reply / reminder needed when reply is pending, or update stage if reply is in.

CEO Office

Step 6. Weekly meeting review

Sync all external meetings, summaries and stage movement; build a weekly meeting memory summary covering which meetings got a follow-up, a reply and a stage move.

CEO Office

Step 7. Monthly meeting lookback

Collect the month's meetings, summaries, current stages and post-meeting communications. Analyse each meeting's fate at the one-month mark — follow-up / reply / stage movement / stall — and build a monthly lookback.

3. Roles & responsibility
CEO Office

Writes / curates every summary, saves meeting memory, tracks post-meeting fate and runs weekly and monthly lookbacks.

Fireflies + GPT script

Transcript source and manual summary generator — only runs when CEO Office triggers them.

FRK / FST / CHK

Passive stores — meeting memory, fund status and tasks only update when CEO Office edits them.

4. Systems (Tools)

  • GCL / FRK — meeting calendars and meeting memory store.
  • Fireflies — transcripts.
  • GPT / script — Russian-language summary generation, run manually.
  • FST — fund status.
  • HubSpot — communication history, follow-up and reply events.
  • Daily Checklist / Task Log (CHK) — post-meeting tasks.

5. Artifacts

  • Meeting summary (RU) — theses, risks, next step, follow-up, open questions.
  • Short notes — what was discussed / agreed / to do next.
  • Meeting memory — saved to FRK (summary, notes, owner, follow-up, next step).
  • Post-meeting tasks — created in CHK.
  • Weekly meeting memory summary and monthly lookback.

7. Pain points
1. Manual summary generation

Every summary requires a human to launch the GPT script or write the notes by hand — high overhead per meeting.

2. No enforced standard

Short notes and summaries drift in structure — theses, risks and next steps are not captured uniformly.

3. Transcript-missing gap

When Fireflies doesn't capture a meeting, meeting memory often ends up incomplete or is dropped entirely.

4. Late post-meeting follow-up

Missed follow-ups surface at the weekly review — often after the window for a timely reminder has closed.

5. Monthly fate inference is manual

Figuring out whether a meeting moved the fund stage requires re-pulling HBS, FRK and FST by hand each month.

Module 04

Notes — Structured Meeting Memory (RU)

Turns transcripts and raw notes into structured Russian-language summaries with theses, risks and next steps — and tracks every meeting's fate at 1 week and 1 month.

Context coverage May 8, 2026 № 04

What this module does

Notes automatically turns transcripts and meeting notes into structured Russian-language summaries with key theses, risks, agreements and next steps. It works even when a full transcript is missing, enforces a single notes standard and links each meeting to follow-ups, tasks, owners and downstream fund-stage movement.

User stories

  1. After a meeting the agent uses the Fireflies transcript to auto-build a Russian summary with theses, risks and next steps — no manual writing.
  2. The agent can assemble a summary from notes or bullets when the transcript is missing — the meeting does not drop out of the process.
  3. See short meeting notes in a single format — recall decisions fast without reading long transcripts.
  4. The agent proposes follow-up, next task and owner from the summary — to move comms forward faster.
  5. The agent shows the fate of the meeting one week and one month later — follow-up, reply, stage movement.

Delivery requirements

  • Fireflies transcript ingestion with fallback to notes / bullets / calendar context.
  • RU structured summary template: theses, risks, open questions, follow-up, next step.
  • Meeting memory object stored in the fundraising calendar and linked to HubSpot.
  • Automated weekly and monthly meeting-fate lookback packs.
  • Post-meeting task creation: summary, follow-up, next step, owner confirmation.
